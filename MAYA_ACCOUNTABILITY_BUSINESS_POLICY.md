# Maya accountability and business-model commitments

Founder-authorized direction — November 4, 2026.

Maya is a living accountability system in development. Invoice screening is one function within its broader purpose: follow evidence, explain what happened, find the leak, assign responsibility, verify the repair and check that it stays fixed.

## Evidence and fairness
- Record who, what, when, where, quantity, value, permission, source references and reasons when those are available. Distinguish event time from recording time and claimed names from verified identities.
- Preserve original in-scope records and visible corrections. Mark facts, claims, disputes, inference and missing evidence. Never fill gaps with invented details.
- An exception or suspicious pattern is a reason to investigate. It is not proof that a person is dishonest. Attribution, intent and misconduct require evidence and accountable review. Preserve contradictory evidence.
- Make relevant evidence visible to authorized reviewers. Transparency does not mean publishing confidential client, donor, employee or resident records.

## Complete correction loop
1. Detect and explain an evidence-supported discrepancy.
2. Assign a named accountable role, due date, severity and evidence needed.
3. Record the correction with supporting source references; retain the original.
4. Have an authorized reviewer verify the actual correction against source-system evidence.
5. Record the preventive control and test it, including legitimate activity that should still pass.
6. Monitor for recurrence and reopen failures. Do not call a fix permanent without evidence that its control continues to work.

## Commercial commitment
Reduce avoidable loss and unresolved work. Do not preserve a defect or perpetuate investigations to generate revenue. Maintain successful controls and grow with the organization’s demonstrated needs. Separate flagged value, disputed amount, verified loss, verified recovery and independently measured savings. Price and describe services honestly.

## Interoperability and scope
Use approved, scoped connections. Start read-only where practical. Never collect a different provider’s password through a Maya form. Each external AI agent or system needs an authenticated adapter, source references, time fields, authorization scope, replay/idempotency controls and documented mapping. A website sign-in is not blanket access to private ChatGPT history, email, banking or company systems.

White Center Food Bank’s public pages confirm Salesforce appointments and CERVIS volunteer registration. They do not establish a Maya connection or reveal the organization’s accounting, inventory or AI systems. Do not claim an unsupported program is deficient based only on its public pages.

## Operational expansion
Delivery scheduling, meeting scheduling, authorized meeting transcription, invoice capture and other pantry workflows are part of the requested product direction. Each requires an implemented workflow, permission model, supported source integration and verification before being offered as working. Verify existing tools’ useful functions before considering replacement.

## Release status

The website also offers a browser-local fictional repair walkthrough with role assignment, correction evidence, review, a sample preventive-control test and simulated reopening. It is not a durable production case-management or enforcement engine.
Under development: organization connectors, verified external approvers, retained source documents for journeys, production assigned-case workflow, verified external remediation, enforced preventive controls and ongoing recurrence monitoring. Delivery scheduling and transcription are not deployed.
No universal connector, all-competitor superiority, independently verified customer savings or decades-long hosting guarantee is claimed. Provider-level disaster recovery remains unverified.

## November 4 implemented report and Android update
The explicit allocation review follows supplied payments to supplied charges, flags unexplained or overallocated amounts, compares dispatched versus received quantities and reports structured instruction mismatches. It does not infer missing allocations or determine intent, competence or misconduct. Source references remain claims until verified against retained original evidence.

A human reviews the exact saved report and chooses Yes or No before file creation; a five-minute grant is rechecked by the server and can be withdrawn. That grant covers the report file only. Sending a report and applying a business-system correction each require separate, specific authority and a verified connector. Neither external action is currently performed.

Native Android demonstration source is merged on main. Its debug APK build/lint passed; native offline fictional report and approved export are implemented. Android device runtime, production signing and Play Store release are not verified. The online account workspace opens in the system browser.

The founder proposes a 30-day free trial followed by an explicitly selected paid plan. Price, final terms, cancellation, billing verification and trial enforcement are unfinished; no recurring billing is activated. Note-retention deadlines are separate from a commercial trial.
