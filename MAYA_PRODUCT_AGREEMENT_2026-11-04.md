# Maya Node product agreement

Founder clarification November 4, 2026. This is the product objective, not a claim that all capabilities are implemented.

Maya must become a living, evidence-linked accountability record for an organization’s products, money, decisions and permissions. Invoice screening is one component, not the product definition. The goal is to identify and explain verified losses and unresolved discrepancies, assign remediation, verify the correction and enforce a rule or process change that addresses recurrence.

## Required complete loop
1. Collect authorized source records from the business and its AI agents, preserving source identity and exact original evidence.
2. Link events, quantities, transactions, price/valuation changes, actors, authorizers, permissions, event time and recording time. Separate reported names from authenticated identities.
3. Reconstruct the story with citations to retained sources. Clearly mark facts, supplied claims, disputes, inferences and missing evidence. Do not manufacture unrecorded details.
4. Reconcile balances and detect duplicate activity, unauthorized changes, unexplained charges, missing stock, expiry and other rule violations. Distinguish total flagged value from the disputed amount, verified loss and verified savings.
5. Open an investigation with a named responsible person, severity, due date and evidence needed. Resolve recipients before any outbound notification; keep confidential evidence private.
6. Record the corrective action without silently overwriting history. Require supporting evidence and an accountable reviewer.
7. Verify the actual correction against its source system. Do not close a case based only on an AI assertion or a reassuring narrative.
8. Record and enforce the preventive rule/process change, then test or monitor for recurrence. A fix is not called permanent merely because an incident was closed.
9. Preserve readable portable archives and verify recovery. A decades-long availability promise needs explicit retention, independent backups, key custody, deletion handling, migration and ongoing operational ownership.

## Current implementation versus objective
Implemented: local invoice CSV check; hosted pantry product events and visible corrections; hash-linked history; JSON export; connected ChatGPT guide; exact-file permission control; signed note backup/restore and isolated hosted recovery drill.
Still not implemented: general organization/source-system connectors, external-agent ingestion identity, independently verified business-authorizer identities, evidence-document archive for journeys, a complete assigned investigation queue, verified external remediation, a configurable preventive-rule engine and ongoing recurrence monitoring. No bank/accounting connector, proven customer loss prevention or universal superiority claim.

The fictional USD2,550 invoice demonstration is NOT a customer loss story. It uses invented sample rows:1200 repeated paid invoice,450 versus300 approval,900 missing approval. Only150 of the450 is above approval. A separate200 invoice is review-ready. No actual company data was accessed to create this example.

The food-pantry example begins an inventory story:100 bags received,60 distributed,5 wasted,35 remain, unit reference value2.00 changes to2.50 with a stated source, a request50 is held. The observed history is not proof of actual physical events or verified financial loss.

## Interoperability
The target is for Maya to receive appropriately authorized evidence from different business AI agents, not only ChatGPT. Current MCP tools and guide are exposed through the Site’s ChatGPT connection. General agent compatibility requires documented authenticated ingestion, source identifiers, idempotency, evidence hashes, permission scope and tested adapters. Do not imply sign-in automatically grants access to a customer’s email, bank, accounting system or private ChatGPT history.

## Bad actors and truth
Flag evidence-supported anomalies and access violations. Treat accusations, intent and attribution as separate matters requiring evidence and human review. Preserve contradictory evidence and corrections. The system must be able to say unknown.
