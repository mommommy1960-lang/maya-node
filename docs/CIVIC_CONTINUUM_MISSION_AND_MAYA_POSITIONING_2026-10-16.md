### Accountability people can use
Building Maya Node to keep automated actions tied to current permission, visible responsibility and a route to correction. Our purpose is to help people understand and challenge systems affecting their lives while reducing the burden of receiving essential services.

## Maya's core, kept intact
Maya separates technical capability from authority: who authorized a particular action, for which purpose, whether that authority is current, who owns the outcome and how an affected person can challenge it. Housing, distribution, banking and education are application settings for this core, not separate certifications or finished integrations.

Legacy Commons node-access/CERL documents describe software/research licensing and access concepts. CUP describes allow-listed keys and signed messages. Neither establishes government accreditation or verifies every AI's legal legitimacy. External credential validation would need a named issuer, current evidence, scope and revocation checks; this capability is not claimed deployed.

## Four mission markets
| Market | Initial workflow to validate | Scope boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Housing and community services | Record correction, advocate permissions, accountable handoffs; later validate rent/utility aid, maintenance and homelessness-prevention workflows | No tenant ranking, eviction action or automated eligibility determination |
| Essential-goods distribution and hygiene services | Anonymous pickup counts, volunteer access and supply handoffs; potential food, clothing, toiletries and shower-service settings | No optional-data requirement for essentials; each program's rules need review |
| Financial services | Purpose-limited summaries, current permission and correction ownership | No real bank integration, credit decision or transfer in the lab |
| Education | Limited record access, expiry and correction review | No student monitoring, discipline, aid award or eligibility decision in the lab |

Account creation, subscriptions and payments are shared workflows across these markets. Revoking payment authorization does not automatically cancel a contract. Broader applications are proposed scope; the fictional pickup and ledger fixtures do not implement every listed service.

## What is demonstrated
The portable lab includes a plain-language action receipt: requested action, purpose, allow/block reason, simulated review owner, current permission, correction status and an explicit statement that external identity/licenses are unverified. Twenty-three local Node functional checks pass against the actual inline JavaScript in a simulated DOM. Earlier 13 core-model and 8 browser-contract checks remain dated evidence. No full browser, security, accessibility or independent audit is claimed.

[Code and evidence under review in PR69](https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/maya-node/pull/69). The prototype runs locally without Cloudflare or a paid AI API. It uses fictional records, editable browser state and simulated review; it makes no real charge, account or service decision. Hosted software remains prelaunch, with deployment/provider billing, identity and payment verification gates open.

## Differentiation without unsupported novelty claims
Consent receipts, authorization engines, human oversight and explainability already exist. Kantara published consent-receipt work years ago; Cerbos and Oso document authorization; Credo AI documents governance; Fiddler documents explainability. This bounded research does not establish that Maya or its combination is unprecedented, patentable or superior.

Our hypothesis to test is whether small essential-service teams and affected people can use a clearly bounded permission, ownership and correction workflow more effectively. Benchmark the same workflow with consenting participants: comprehension, time to identify an owner, permission mistakes, acknowledged requests, completed corrections and delivery cost. No competitor product was tested.

## Lessons from other builders
- Amazon's 1997 letter emphasizes a focused starting market, customer value, repeat purchases, word of mouth and spending discipline. Our next proof is a repeatable useful outcome, not worldwide brand recognition by a promised date.
- Code for America connects human-centered benefits delivery with service partners and measured outcomes. We need actual partner evidence beyond synthetic permission tests.
- Findhelp documents referrals, outcome tracking and integration into existing workflows. Maya should test a complementary control/correction layer rather than immediately duplicating a nationwide directory.
- Cerbos documents policy separation, integration and audit operations. Oso's educational examples make a technical problem understandable. Our lab still needs protected server enforcement and a concise operator/developer path before real data.

These are desk-research lessons from organizations at different stages. Vendor claims and reported impact were not independently reproduced.
