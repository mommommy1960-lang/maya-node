# Civic Continuum: Five-Part Clarity and Accountability Standard

Status: Founder-directed standing communication policy, adopted November 3, 2026.
Scope: Civic Continuum correspondence, customer-facing communications, contracts and proposals, operational requests, and instructions to AI agents, including Maya Node, SAGE, and Aurora. This is a drafting and review standard; it does not by itself prove that any deployed agent enforces it.

## Mandatory drafting and review checklist
1. **Primary instruction:** State the first-choice request and desired outcome in plain language.
2. **Conditional alternatives:** Define fallback options and their exact triggering conditions. A conditional alternative does not replace the primary request unless expressly authorized.
3. **Prohibited interpretations:** State what the communication does *not* mean and what changes, cancellations, disclosures, payments, or substitutions are not authorized.
4. **Required actions:** Identify the responsible person or system, specific steps, deadlines where appropriate, and how exceptions are handled.
5. **Confirmation requirements:** Require written acknowledgement of the request, its conditions, and any material decision or change before action when practicable.

## Safeguard
If any instruction is ambiguous, seek clarification before taking a consequential or irreversible action affecting rights, housing, opportunities, money, privacy, services, or safety. Do not silently convert a conditional request into a different election or authorization. Preserve the original request, clarification, decision, reason, timing, and confirmation in an auditable record, subject to privacy and retention rules.

## Drafting practice
Before sending important correspondence, the assistant should proactively flag any missing category and offer a concise revision. The five categories may appear as explicit headings for high-stakes correspondence or be integrated naturally into ordinary messages. Avoid condescending language and needless verbosity. Do not claim a recipient's intent or misconduct as proven without evidence.

## Origin and purpose
This standard was directed by the founder after a November 3, 2026 discussion about an October 23 housing letter that prioritized a two-bedroom detached home and conditionally requested consideration of a three-bedroom accommodation. A subsequent appointment cancellation was reportedly justified by an interpretation of that conditional language. The case is a motivating example, not a verified adjudication of wrongdoing. The standard is designed to reduce ambiguity and make decision-making accountable.

## Bruce-to-Bruce handoff
At the start of any consequential drafting or operational task, recall and apply: **primary instruction; conditional alternatives; prohibited interpretations; required actions; confirmation requirements**. Ask for clarification before materially changing the primary instruction. Distinguish a requested alternative from an authorized change. Record decision and confirmation. This handoff should be copied into other project contexts as appropriate; a GitHub document alone cannot guarantee cross-chat recall or runtime enforcement.
