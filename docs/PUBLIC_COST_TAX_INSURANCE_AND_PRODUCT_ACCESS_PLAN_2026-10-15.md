## Taxes: included in planning, not guessed as a flat amount
Taxes depend on actual activity, customer location, sales type, revenue, deductions, and entity/tax treatment. The $682 is not a tax-inclusive all-in estimate.
- Washington B&O tax is generally based on gross receipts, not profit; classifications and rates depend on the activity. The WA Department of Revenue explains the tax here: https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/business-occupation-tax
- Some businesses may qualify for small-business credits or non-reporting treatment, but eligibility and filing requirements must be checked. Do not assume zero tax or zero filing merely because revenue is small.
- Seattle’s 2026 B&O tax threshold is generally $2 million of annual taxable revenue, but annual reporting can still be required even when no tax is due. See: https://www.seattle.gov/city-finance/business-taxes-and-licenses/business-taxes
- Sales tax may apply to certain retail sales, digital products, or services depending on what is sold and applicable rules. Determine classification before selling.
- No tax amount is included in the baseline because projected taxable receipts and final service classification have not been confirmed. Add tax due as a separate actual/forecast line once those facts are known.

## Product access: recommended separation of demo, free trial, and $25 offer
This is the recommended product ladder for discussion and implementation—not a claim that the hosted service is currently working or that checkout is enabled.

1. **Five-minute public demo — no account/payment required.** A guided, disposable sandbox with a few preset actions. Show one allowed task, one blocked overreach, revoke access, and an audit/event summary. Use fictional data only. Reset automatically; no live customer systems or real personal/financial/health/student records.
2. **One-hour free trial — limited interactive sandbox.** Give a user a genuine chance to operate the software for up to 60 minutes, with clear start/end time, usage limits, fictional/sample data, reset, and a visible notice that alerts/identities/integrations are simulated where applicable. Do not silently collect sensitive data. Trial expiry should stop further use cleanly; do not promise production-grade integrations.
3. **$25 Founding Review — human-guided, bounded offer.** The current documented offer is a $25 bounded human review, not an unrestricted paid software subscription. State exactly what the customer receives, delivery window, what is excluded, refund/cancellation terms, and how to contact a human. Keep checkout/sales disabled until the complete purchase-to-delivery path, payment webhooks, quota handling, refund handling, abuse controls, and support process are verified.
4. **Paid software plan — separate future decision.** Do not conflate the $25 review with a recurring subscription. Publish price, billing interval, limits, cancellation/refund policy, privacy/security scope, and support terms before enabling it.

## Demo acceptance checklist
- User can operate the five-minute demo, not just view a slide or screenshot.
- The four sector scenarios (school, banking, nonprofit, customer support) run in a sandbox using fictional data.
- Allowed read works; excessive export is denied; revoke causes subsequent read to fail; correction/review action is clearly labeled as simulated if it is not connected to a real workflow.
- Reset restores the scenario; status messages are accessible; mobile layout and keyboard navigation are tested.
- No real system, real identity provider, live alert service, or production AI integration is implied unless independently verified.
- The one-hour trial has a real timer/expiry and meaningful sandbox actions; tests record start, expiry, reset, and access enforcement.
- Evidence distinguishes source review/local tests from hosted browser tests. Never call source inspection a live end-to-end test.
