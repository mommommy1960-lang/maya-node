# Maya Node Early-Access Pilot Policy — Founder-Approved Baseline
**Decision date:** 2026-10-25  
**Status:** Baseline approved by the founder in ChatGPT; operational prerequisites remain open.  
**Important:** This approval sets the proposed internal baseline. It is not a privacy-policy publication, legal review, service-level agreement, partner consent, or authorization to process live customer data.

## Approved baseline decisions

1. **Retention target:** 30 days for pilot request records, outputs, and associated receipts, measured from creation, unless a shorter period is required. The implementation must be checked before promising automatic expiry; until verified, disclose that records may persist until owner deletion.
2. **Backup expiry target:** Backups should expire no later than 30 additional days after the primary record's deletion/expiry, but only if the actual hosting/storage configuration supports and verifies this. Do not claim backup deletion is implemented until evidenced.
3. **Incident channel:** A monitored business email is the intended reporting channel. The exact address must be entered and tested before a pilot starts; no mailbox integration or delivery path is currently confirmed.
4. **Primary human owner:** Founder/project owner. The specific display name and monitored contact must be written into the final operational copy before launch.
5. **Backup human owner:** Still unassigned. A real person and monitored contact must be selected before a pilot starts.
6. **Response expectation:** Acknowledge an incident report within one business day during the stated support window. This is an acknowledgement target, not a promise to resolve the incident within one business day. No 24/7 coverage is promised.

## Partner permission and launch gate

No partner permission is granted by this internal founder approval. Before any live business/customer data is used, obtain written permission from the participating business that identifies the workflow, data categories, permitted actions, users, retention/deletion terms, incident channel, and exit procedure.

Until all of the following are evidenced, use synthetic or properly redacted test data only:
- hosted workflow and current deployment independently re-verified;
- customer-controlled record review and deletion tested end-to-end;
- receipt completeness and deletion behavior verified in current tests;
- retention and backup expiry claims matched to actual configuration;
- monitored incident channel tested;
- primary and backup human contacts named;
- written partner scope and permission recorded.

## Approval record and limits

The founder approved the proposed baseline in the ChatGPT conversation on 2026-10-25. This record captures that decision; it does not claim that technical controls already enforce these targets. Any mismatch between the target and actual system behavior must be disclosed and resolved before live data is accepted.
