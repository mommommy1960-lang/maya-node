# Maya Node Founding Review

Maya Node is a focused design-review service for founders, builders, and researchers who want a plain-English second look at consent, permission boundaries, authority escalation, auditability, and fail-closed behavior in an AI design.

## What the founding review includes

For the first three clients, the one-time founding review is **$25** and includes:

- A written boundary-risk report.
- One practical adversarial test aimed at the design's stated limits.
- One written clarification response about the delivered findings.

This is a design review. It is not certification, legal advice, penetration testing, a safety guarantee, production approval, a claim of sentience, or a claim that the reviewed system is production-ready.

1. Read the scope and boundaries.

4. Submit only the minimum non-secret, privacy-safe material needed for review.
5. Receive the report and adversarial-test record, with a target of five business days after complete intake and usable materials are received.
6. Use the included written clarification response.
