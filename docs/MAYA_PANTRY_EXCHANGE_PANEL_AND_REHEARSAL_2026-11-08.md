# Maya Node pantry exchange rehearsal and five-panel review

**Date:** November 8, 2026. **Scope:** Fictional exercise, public-source budget context, and modeled comparisons. This record contains no partner data or partner commitments.

## What the coordinator sees

Open [the Maya pantry board](https://maya-working-workflow-lab.myqueen1960.chatgpt.site/food-bank#pantry-board). The 2:00 a.m. replay opens with five precomputed opportunities. The coordinator reads the sender, recipient, amount, timing, driver, any storage stop and approval status, then chooses yes or no. Changing replay time demonstrates a new plan or a hold. The public replay uses isolated sample teams and simulated identities. Decisions are in browser memory. It can be opened day or night. Real feeds, unattended processing of partner inventory, notifications and dispatch require an authorized production rollout.

## Audited cash spending and editable basket

- [West Seattle Food Bank FY2025 audited functional expenses, PDF p. 7](https://westseattlefoodbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-WSFB-Final-Financial-Statements.pdf) reports $833,397 of purchased food. A hypothetical 1% replacement is $8,333.97 gross.
- [Ballard Food Bank FY2024 audit, functional expenses](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5d4b363f0341aa0001f5c17b/t/68420e682649926f391dec42/1749159529597/Ballard%2BFood%2BBank%2B-%2BFinal%2B2024%2BAudit.pdf) reports $2,118,857 of food purchases. A hypothetical 1% replacement is $21,188.57 gross.
- [USDA AMS FY2026 bulk purchase table](https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/AMSPurchasesbyCommodityFY2026.pdf) gives expenditure and weight for dry pinto beans (12 × 2 lb), US #2 long-grain rice (24 × 2 lb) and fresh oranges (34–39 lb carton). Ratios are rounded to $0.62, $0.38 and $0.78 per pound. These are national procurement benchmarks, not the two pantries’ prices.

Worked example: 180 lb beans, 180 lb rice, 120 lb oranges per active week for 48 weeks. Gross avoided purchases $13,132.80 less $2,160 transport, storage and paid labor and $1,200 annual setup, support and software. Net $9,772.80 per year, averaging $814.40 per month, $187.94 per calendar week or $26.77 per calendar day. Quantities and costs are assumptions. Count usable arrivals that cancel or reduce a planned purchase and use that purchase’s invoice price. Count food rescued and volunteer time separately. Sum all parties’ added costs and avoid counting the same food on both sides. The two public audits establish spending scale and give no evidence of actual Maya savings.

## Partner authorization before real operation

The [partner sign-off checklist](https://maya-working-workflow-lab.myqueen1960.chatgpt.site/maya-transfer-readiness.txt) is a preparation template. Use distinct organizational identities and verified authority for sending, storing, transporting and receiving. Document the coordinator’s permitted action and named escalation lead and backup. Capture item and lot, count, labels and allergens, temperature or handling evidence, custody at each stop, windows, and the approved version. Stop on missing evidence, stale supply, unavailable capacity, or a safety event. A change to the approved plan requires new confirmation. Washington’s [charity food donation guidance](https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/food/food-worker-and-industry/charity-food-donations) is a starting reference for partner review. Real data, real dispatch, and live billing remain gated.
