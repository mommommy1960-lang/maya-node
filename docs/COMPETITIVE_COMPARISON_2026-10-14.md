## Our defensible strengths
1. A clearly bounded **$25 human review** provides a specific small starting engagement. It is not a platform license or a claim that enterprise deployment costs less than competitors.
2. **Public inspectable proof:** code snapshots, actual test runs, selected public data, reproducible aggregation and decision records, and preserved failures/corrections are linked together.
3. **Plain-language human-authority walkthrough:** allowed scope, excessive export, revocation, named response owner and separately reviewed correction are visible for four buyer groups.

These are positioning strengths, not proven uniqueness. The inspected sources do not establish that competitors lack these features. Our six public-data assertions and 1129 software tests do not establish that our product is more accurate, safer, faster or more secure than those platforms.

## What competitors document that we have not established
The competitor materials describe broader inventories, commercial integrations and enterprise risk/control operations. Our current paid service is human-delivered and our demonstrations have narrower local scope. Production scaling, protected multi-user evidence, live transport/identity, deployment and independent assurance remain open.
