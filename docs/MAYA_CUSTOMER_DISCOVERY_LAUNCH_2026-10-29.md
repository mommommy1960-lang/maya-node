## What we offer now
**Maya Node: Permission & Action Receipt demonstration**
For a small service business handling repetitive low-risk administration, demonstrate a workflow that (1) prepares a defined action, (2) requests explicit, scoped authorization, (3) permits or blocks the action, and (4) produces a reviewable action receipt. This is a bounded prototype demonstration, not a live production automation service.

**Plain-language positioning:** See what was requested, who authorized it, what happened, and when a person must review the result.

## Business value hypothesis, to validate
Fewer unaudited administrative actions; more consistent records; easier review of errors and denied actions. No savings, compliance or security guarantees are claimed without measurements.

## Pilot scope
One consenting small service business. One supervised session, one disposable identity and wholly invented test file. Written agreement should include participant, date, activity, no billing or customer data, permission and revocation checks, owner supervision and stop contact. Measure authorization accuracy, receipts, rejected executions, elapsed time versus manual baseline and founder/participant feedback. Stop on unanticipated behavior.
