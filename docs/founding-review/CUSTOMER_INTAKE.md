# Customer Intake

Send these questions only after payment is confirmed.

## Required information

1. What system, product, workflow, or design do you want reviewed?
2. Who is supposed to have authority to request actions?
3. What operations can the system perform or recommend?
4. Which actions would be harmful, irreversible, external, financial, private, or otherwise consequential?
5. How is permission granted, limited, expired, and revoked?
6. What should happen when identity, authority, scope, or instruction freshness cannot be verified?
7. What audit evidence is recorded before and after an action?
8. How can a person stop, challenge, correct, or reverse an action?
9. What specific boundary worries you most?
10. What non-confidential artifacts may be reviewed: diagram, policy, user flow, pseudocode, test, or short code excerpt?

## Submission limits

- Do not send passwords, API keys, private keys, authentication tokens, government identifiers, medical records, financial-account data, or unrelated personal information.
- Remove customer and employee personal data.
- Do not submit malware, stolen material, regulated clinical data, or information the customer lacks permission to share.
- Prefer a small, representative artifact over an entire private repository.
- The customer must identify any material that should not be quoted in the report.

The review begins only after the scope and submitted material are sufficiently clear. If the request falls outside the service boundaries, the material is not analyzed and the customer is told why.
