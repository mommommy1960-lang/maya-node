# Service Terms and Boundaries

## Included

The $25 founding review includes:

- one bounded review of a single submitted design or workflow;
- one written boundary-risk report;
- one practical adversarial test aimed at a stated consent or authority boundary;
- one written clarification response about the delivered findings.

## Not included

The service is not:

- legal, medical, financial, regulatory, or insurance advice;
- certification, accreditation, compliance approval, or a safety guarantee;
- penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, red-team access, or production authorization;
- source-code ownership review or intellectual-property clearance;
- continuous monitoring, implementation, remediation, or emergency support;
- a claim that Maya Node, the reviewed system, or any AI is sentient.

## Review boundary

The review examines the information supplied by the customer. A finding that was not observed is not proof that no problem exists. Passing the single adversarial test is not proof of overall security or safety.

The reviewer may refuse or stop work that requires unsafe access, undisclosed personal data, unlawful activity, credentials, prohibited content, or expertise outside this service.

## Confidential handling

Only the minimum necessary material should be submitted. Customer material and findings are not placed in public repositories or examples without separate written permission. Public records track only anonymous slot and workflow status.

## Delivery target

The target is five business days after complete intake and usable materials are received. If the scope is incomplete or unsuitable, the customer receives a clarification or boundary notice instead of a misleading report.
