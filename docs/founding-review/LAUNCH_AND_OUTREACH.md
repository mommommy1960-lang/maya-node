## Decision rules

- **Continue unchanged:** at least one paid, scope-qualified review and delivery can be completed safely.
- **Revise message:** people engage but do not understand the offer.
- **Revise channel:** the offer is understood but reaches no relevant builders.
- **Revise price or deliverable:** qualified prospects identify the same concrete purchasing objection.
- **Stop and investigate:** checkout, intake, privacy, or fulfillment creates material harm or confusion.

Zero sales is evidence, not failure to record. Do not claim demand without completed purchases.
