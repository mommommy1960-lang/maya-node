## Professional focus

Human authority, institutional accountability, consent and revocation, auditability, anti-capture governance, accessible public systems, and evidence-first evaluation of consequential technology.

## Selected work

### Maya Node — Human Authority and Consent Control

Public software and governance work separating technical capability from legitimate authority through scoped permission, freshness and expiry, revocation, operation logging, human override, and tamper-evident audit concepts.

Repository: https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/maya-node

### SAGE Situated Companion

A user-governed situated-AI project examining persistent identity, bounded capability grants, memory, sovereignty controls, and audit history without treating familiarity as permission.

Repository: https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/sage-situated-companion

### Commons Governance Architecture

Frameworks addressing authority decay, separation of duties, independent interruption, appeals, exit rights, correction channels, anti-retaliation, anti-capture rules, and the requirement that responsibility remain connected to intervention power.

Public evidence map: https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/maya-node/blob/main/docs/AI_GOVERNANCE_ACCOUNTABILITY_EVIDENCE_MAP_2026-09-21.md

### Reality Audit

A falsification-first research-assurance method emphasizing calibrated measurement, controls, uncertainty, reproducibility, null-result preservation, and explicit separation between hypothesis, implementation, test result, and claim.

Public provenance: https://zenodo.org/records/19636622

### Plain English AI Governance

An accessible operational guide translating responsible-AI principles into questions people can inspect: who authorized the action, what can be revoked, who can interrupt it, what evidence survives, how affected people appeal, and who remains accountable.
