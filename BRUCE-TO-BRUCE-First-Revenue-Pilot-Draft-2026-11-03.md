## Objective
Validate whether one small business will pay for a founder-led AI Privacy & Accuracy Checkup without depending on production Maya Node software.

## Proposed service
A human-led, educational review of a business's public-facing AI-generated content or fictional/redacted sample workflows. No confidential customer data, personal records, login credentials, or access to private systems. No security penetration testing, legal compliance certification, or guaranteed outcomes.

Deliverables (proposal):
1. 30-minute discovery call using non-sensitive descriptions.
2. Review of up to three publicly available or synthetic examples for factual accuracy, privacy exposure, and human oversight gaps.
3. Two-page findings memo: prioritized issues, suggested corrections, and a 30-day action checklist.
4. One 20-minute findings discussion.

Proposed introductory fixed fee: $250, subject to founder approval, business registration/terms and payment readiness. No fee has been approved or charged.

## Proposed safeguards
- Written scope and plain-language limitations before engagement.
- Customer must not send sensitive information; stop and delete unintended sensitive submissions under an approved handling process.
- No production Maya Node, Sage, or Aurora processing of customer data.
- Do not promise certified audits, legal advice, regulatory compliance, insurance, or guaranteed security.
- Establish refund/cancellation terms, delivery timeline, and customer consent before charging.
- Check business licensing and payment account readiness before commercial acceptance.

Ask whether they would value a fixed-scope checkup and what outcome matters. Record responses and only proceed to a paid pilot after founder approves offer and terms.
