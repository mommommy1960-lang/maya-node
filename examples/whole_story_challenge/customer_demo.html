Records are joined by fictional order ID. Source references are shown with each order so a reviewer can trace the result back to the supplied evidence.

Rules used in this example

1Flag carrier pickup later than the WMS promised-ship-by time.
2Flag WMS and carrier dispatch or delivery timestamps that differ by more than five minutes.
3Show complaints and finance transactions as associated records, not proof of cause.
4Show a missing carrier row as a dataset gap—not proof that physical delivery did not happen.
Financial caution: $137.00 is the sum of recorded refunds and credits linked to the three flagged order IDs. This dataset proves $0.00 of that amount was caused by the discrepancies. No recovered savings or ROI is claimed.
